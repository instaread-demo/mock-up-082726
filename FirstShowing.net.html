"I want the big story, and then I want the bigger story." "I promised these sources they were going to be safe." Sony Pictures has revealed the official full trailer for The Social Reckoning, the highly anticipated follow-up to David Fincher's 2010 film The Social Network about Facebook. The big reveal trailer following the teaser a few months ago featuring way more footage. Screenwriter Aaron Sorkin returns steering the ship, also directing in addition to writing. "The verdict is in. The reckoning is coming." This is essentially the sequel to The Social Network, arriving in theaters this October. "Every revolution begins with a reckoning." This sequel follows the story of Frances Haugen, a young Facebook engineer, who enlists the help of Jeff Horwitz, a Wall Street Journal reporter, to blow the whistle on the social network's most guarded secrets - soon to be revealed. Starring Mikey Madison, Jeremy Allen White, Wunmi Mosaku, Betty Gilpin, Billy Magnussen, with Bill Burr, and Jeremy Strong as the slimy Mark Zuckerberg trying to push back against all the harm that his website has caused. A much better trailer than before, but still a tad hesitant about going all in on it. Though it does look like it's going to hit back hard against Zuckerberg. Take a look.

You can rewatch the teaser trailer for Sorkin's The Social Reckoning movie right here for the first look.

A companion piece to the hit film The Social Network, Sorkin’s original screenplay is based on the events that gave rise to the Wall Street Journal’s shocking exposé The Facebook Files. The film is inspired by the true story of how Frances Haugen (Mikey Madison), a young Facebook engineer, enlists the help of Jeff Horwitz (Jeremy Allen White), a Wall Street Journal reporter, to go on a dangerous journey that ends up blowing the whistle on the social network’s most guarded secrets. The Social Reckoning is both written and directed American writer / filmmaker Aaron Sorkin, director of the movies Molly's Game, The Trial of the Chicago 7, and Being the Ricardos previously, and writer of the original The Social Network. Based on articles written by Jeff Horwitz. Produced by Todd Black, Peter Rice, Aaron Sorkin, Stuart Besser. Sony Pics debuts Sorkin's The Social Reckoning in US theaters starting October 9th, 2026 this fall. Your thoughts?
