Community Champions Awards Highly Commended: Salim Ahmed

Salim Ahmed has been highly commended in the Personal Achievement category for his volunteer work with Lewisham Borough FC. 

The Executive Services Support Analyst at dmg media Tech has spent four years coaching children at his local club. 

DMGT is donating £5,000 to the club to help improve facilities, purchase equipment and support pitch hire costs supporting the local community.

Here’s Salim’s story in his own words:

Lewisham Borough FC is a community football club in South London, supporting more than 300 players across its youth teams and first team.

I’ve been involved with the club for three years and am now going into my fourth season. I started as a coach, progressed to Lead Coach and now serve as Assistant Manager, working with the first team and U15s.

After injuries brought my own playing career to an end, coaching gave me the opportunity to stay involved in football and help young people develop through the game. A big inspiration has been our manager, Lloyd Davis, who encouraged me to get into coaching and has played a key role in my development.

I volunteer more than 12 hours a week, helping with training sessions, matchdays and supporting players and parents wherever I can. While football is the focus, a big part of the role is helping young people build confidence, discipline and resilience.

The 23/24 season was a memorable one for the club. Lewisham Borough recorded its highest-ever league finish, reached the play-offs and won the Division 1 Cup.

The club provides young people with a positive environment where they can develop both on and off the pitch. Over the years, several players have progressed into senior and professional football, including Khiani Shombe, who went on to Southampton U23s before joining Hull City.

DMGT’s £5,000 donation will help fund improved facilities, equipment and pitch hire, giving players the best possible environment to train and develop.

Receiving this award means a great deal because so much of the work happens behind the scenes and on a voluntary basis. I also see it as recognition of the club and the community around it.

Lewisham Borough has become a place that feels like home, and I’m grateful to DMGT for recognising the work we do and supporting the club’s future.
