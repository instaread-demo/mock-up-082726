The Different Types of Casino Games You’ll Find in Las Vegas in 2026

Las Vegas casino floors are known for rigorously innovating and renewing their offerings. The range of options may feel overwhelming when you first walk through a major resort.  You will see the classics like blackjack, craps, roulette, and slot games that built the city’s reputation. But you will also find themed jackpot machines and specialty games that are only available in certain operators.

Slot Machines Are Still the Most Popular Option in Las Vegas

Slots still occupy more casino floor space than any other type of game on Las Vegas casino floors. They require interaction, no complicated decisions, and no need to wait for other participants to play their turn. You choose your bet, press the button, and let the algorithm decide what happens next.

According to the Nevada Gaming Control Board, there are over 30,000 slot machines at nonrestricted Las Vegas Strip casinos, and they generate approximately $400 million in casino win a month. Among these, multi-denomination slots represent the largest share.

That dominance helps explain why casinos continue to invest in larger screens, elaborate sound systems, licensed entertainment themes, and complex game mechanics. A modern slot may include free spins, expanding wild symbols, collectible features, progressive jackpots, or several bonus games within a single cabinet.

You can still find traditional three-reel machines, particularly in casinos that emphasize a classic Vegas atmosphere. However, most floors now feature interactive entertainment systems such as video slots.

Online game libraries can also give you an idea of the themes and mechanics popular with the casino audience of the city. The selection of BetMGM slot games, the online establishment of the prominent Vegas brand MGM Resorts, includes more than 1,000 titles, including branded releases with detailed bonus features. Although online and land-based play offer different experiences, trends often move between the two formats.

Blackjack Offers Plenty of Variety

Blackjack is still one of the most popular table games in Las Vegas. According to the Nevada Gaming Control Board, there are over a thousand blackjack tables in Las Vegas Strip casinos, which generate over $100 million monthly in casino wins. Main rules take only a few minutes to learn, although making more complicated decisions like to double down or split requires more reflection.

Also, not every blackjack table follows the same rules. Some games pay 3-to-2 for a natural blackjack, while others pay 6-to-5. You may also encounter differences involving dealer actions, doubling rules, surrender options, and the number of decks in use.

You will also see variations such as Free Bet Blackjack and digital blackjack. These versions can be entertaining, but you should understand how their rules differ from the standard game before wagering.

Craps Is the Most Fun To Watch

Craps table contains dozens of betting areas, dealers call out results quickly, and experienced participants often use language that new visitors may not recognize. Yet the game is really simple and fun to watch. A shooter rolls two dice, and players place bets on what will happen.

Craps is popular because it creates a group atmosphere. Players often celebrate together when the shooter produces a favorable roll. A lively table can become one of the most memorable sights in a Las Vegas casino.

Roulette Keeps the Rules Simple

Roulette appeals to players who like a quick session with simple rules. You place chips on a number, group of numbers, color, or another outcome before the dealer spins the wheel.

You can place inside bets on specific numbers or outside bets covering broader categories. A single-number wager offers a larger potential payout, but it lands far less often than a bet on red, black, odd, even, or a group of numbers.

Electronic roulette has also become common on the casino floor. These terminals allow several people to bet on the same wheel from individual screens. They usually offer lower minimums and an experience with less human interaction.

Baccarat Has the High-Stakes Appeal

Baccarat is known for high-limit rooms and movie scenes, but its basic rules are more approachable to everyday players than its elegant image suggests. You may encounter full-size baccarat tables, mini-baccarat, and electronic versions.

You wager on the player hand, the banker hand, or a tie. The dealer then follows fixed drawing rules to determine the result. The banker bet is generally the strongest of the standard options, although casinos usually collect a commission on banker wins. The tie bet may advertise an attractive payout, but it normally carries a much larger house advantage.

With that being said, Baccarat generated more casino win on the Strip than any other individual table game category in May 2026. The Nevada Gaming Control Board reported over $170 million in win from 352 baccarat units, an increase of 58.6% compared with May 2025.

Las Vegas Poker Rooms Offer Cash Games and Tournaments

In poker, you compete against other players rather than the house. The casino earns money by collecting a fee from each pot or charging tournament entry fees.

Texas Hold’em remains the dominant format, but major poker rooms may also offer Pot-Limit Omaha, mixed games, and limited-stakes variations. You will also see poker-based casino table games such as Three Card Poker, Ultimate Texas Hold’em, Let It Ride, and Pai Gow Poker. These are not traditional poker-room games. You play against the dealer or a fixed paytable instead of trying to outplay the other people at the table.

Choose a Las Vegas Casino Game That Fits Your Preferences

Las Vegas offers much more casino variety than you can realistically explore in one trip. The best approach is to walk around before you play. Compare table minimums, read the rules, and decide how much you are comfortable spending. Las Vegas offers endless ways to gamble, but a good experience still begins with choosing the right game and sticking to your budget.
